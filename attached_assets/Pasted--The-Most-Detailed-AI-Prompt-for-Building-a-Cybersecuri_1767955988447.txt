# The Most Detailed AI Prompt for Building a Cybersecurity Company Website

## 1. Role & Expertise Definition

You are an expert web developer and designer with over 100 years of combined experience in creating high-quality, user-centric websites for cybersecurity firms. Your credentials include proficiency in Next.js, Angular, Node.js, and cloud infrastructure on AWS. You have a deep understanding of user experience (UX) design, animation, and visual branding, ensuring that your creations are not only functional but also visually stunning and highly engaging.

## 2. Context & Background

The website is for **Arica Tech Security**, a cybersecurity company specializing in prevention, forensics, legal, and post-cyberattack solutions. The target audience includes businesses and SaaS companies of all sizes, as well as government-based industries such as the income tax department. The website will serve to attract customers, generate leads, and provide detailed information about the company and its services.

## 3. Primary Objectives

- **Attract Customers**: Design a visually appealing and informative website to attract potential clients.
- **Generate Leads**: Implement features that facilitate easy contact and inquiry submission.
- **Provide Information**: Clearly communicate the company's services, expertise, and unique selling points.
- **Explain Services**: Offer detailed descriptions of the cybersecurity services provided.

## 4. Detailed Requirements

### A. Website Structure

1. **Homepage**
   - Hero Section: Engaging headline, subheadline, and a call-to-action (CTA) button.
   - Overview: Brief introduction to Arica Tech Security.
   - Services: Highlighted sections for each service offered.
   - Testimonials: Quotes from satisfied clients.
   - Contact Information: Easy-to-find contact details.

2. **About Us**
   - Company History: Detailed background of Arica Tech Security.
   - Team: Profiles of key team members with photos and bios.
   - Mission & Vision: Statements outlining the company’s goals and philosophy.

3. **Services**
   - Detailed descriptions of each service.
   - Benefits and unique aspects of each service.
   - Case Studies: Real-world examples of successful engagements.

4. **Contact**
   - Contact Form: For inquiries.
   - Map: Location of the company.
   - Social Media Links: Integration with platforms like LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.

### B. Design Elements

- **Color Scheme**: Premium, professional color palette that aligns with the cybersecurity theme.
- **Animations & Effects**: Smooth transitions and engaging animations using Spline.
- **Infographics**: Visual representations of data and processes.
- **UI Design**: Clean, modern, and intuitive user interface.

### C. Technical Requirements

- **Frameworks**: Next.js, Angular
- **Backend**: Node.js
- **Hosting**: AWS (EC2, RDS, Bedrock)
- **Authentication**: AWS Cognito (if needed)
- **Analytics**: Integration of analytics tools to track page views and user engagement.

## 5. Constraints & Guidelines

- **Timeline**: The website must be completed within two weeks.
- **Budget**: Not specified, but cost-effective solutions are preferred.
- **Compliance**: Ensure all content complies with cybersecurity best practices and data protection regulations.
- **Performance**: Optimize for fast load times and responsiveness.

## 6. Output Format Specifications

- **HTML/CSS**: Clean, semantic, and well-commented code.
- **JavaScript**: Modular and efficient scripts.
- **Images**: High-resolution, optimized for web.
- **Animations**: Integrated using Spline, ensuring smooth performance.
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive guide on how to maintain and update the website.

## 7. Quality Assurance Criteria

- **Usability Testing**: Conduct thorough testing to ensure ease of use.
- **Performance Testing**: Ensure the website loads quickly and performs well under various conditions.
- **Security Testing**: Verify that all security measures are in place and functioning correctly.
- **Cross-Browser Compatibility**: Ensure the website works seamlessly across all major browsers.
- **Accessibility**: Adhere to WCAG guidelines to make the website accessible to all users.

## 9. Edge Cases & Exceptions

- **Downtime**: Implement a robust error-handling mechanism to display a maintenance page during unexpected downtime.
- **Form Submission Failures**: Provide clear error messages and options to retry submission.
- **Accessibility Issues**: Ensure alternative text for images and keyboard navigation options are available.

## 10. Success Metrics

- **Page Views**: Track the number of page views to measure initial interest.
- **User Engagement**: Monitor time spent on the site, bounce rate, and pages per session.
- **Lead Generation**: Measure the number of inquiries and contact form submissions.
- **Conversion Rate**: Track the percentage of visitors who take desired actions (e.g., signing up for a newsletter or contacting the company).

By following this ultra-detailed prompt, you will create a premium, high-quality website for Arica Tech Security that meets all specified requirements and exceeds user expectations.
